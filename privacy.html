Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy explains what Titik Temu Studio stores when it is connected to TikTok. The app is used only by its operator, so the only TikTok account it handles is the operator's own.

Data the app receives from TikTok

  • An access token and a refresh token, used to send videos to the operator's TikTok drafts.
  • The account's open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar), used to show which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only in the operator's private Google Drive folder used by the app. They are not stored on any third-party server run by the operator, and they are never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Other data

The app processes video files and their transcripts to make clips. These files stay in the operator's private storage. The app does not collect data about TikTok viewers, followers, or any other person.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the operator uses the app. Disconnecting the app in TikTok's settings, or deleting the app's Google Drive folder, removes the stored tokens.
