Terms of Service

Effective 6 November 2026

Titik Temu Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by Firdian Zaki Putra Wibowo for the TikTok account Titik Temu. It helps the operator edit short Islamic reminder clips and send them as drafts to his own TikTok account.

Who may use the app

The app is for the operator's personal use only. It has a single password-protected account. No public sign-up is offered, and no other person may use the app.

What the app does with TikTok

  • When the operator connects TikTok, the app asks for the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions.
  • The app only uploads videos that the operator chooses, to the operator's own TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted publicly without the operator opening TikTok and posting it himself.
  • The app does not read, like, comment on, follow, or message any TikTok user.

Content responsibility

The operator is responsible for every video he uploads and for following TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Clips that come from other creators are credited to their source in the video and its caption.

Availability

The app is provided as is, for personal use, without any guarantee of availability. It may be changed or switched off at any time.

Disconnecting

The connection can be removed at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
